# Wagent Ai

## Register
product

## Users
Independent traders discover declarative agents; creators build, test, and sell strategies. One MetaMask wallet identity supports both workflows. Administrators are explicitly configured by wallet address and review each strategy version. Email/password accounts and reset emails are removed.

## Product Purpose
Create. Sell. Buy once. Run your agent. Purchase grants perpetual personal use of a specific version, with a self-hosted runtime and buyer-managed credentials. The complete local demo must be executable and clearly identified.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, independent. A useful marketplace with credible detail and no profitability promises.

## Anti-references
No subscription upsells, invented verified returns, casino styling, or ambiguous live-trading controls. Avoid clutter, decorative glass, and excessive accent colors.

## Design Principles
Make one-time pricing explicit. Keep operating costs and license terms close to purchase. Distinguish simulation everywhere. Let buyers understand a strategy before using it. Give every action a visible result.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive mobile workflows.
