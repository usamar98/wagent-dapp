# Wagent Ai

## Register
product

## Users
People connect their own personal WhatsApp agent to a strategy they create or license. One MetaMask identity supports the command desk, marketplace, and creator workflows. Administrators review strategy versions.

## Product Purpose
Your strategy, one conversation away. Build or buy a versioned agent, pair the personal WhatsApp chat through its official Agent Platform API, and control it from either chat or the website. Crypto command orders use expiring previews and explicit confirmations. The current execution engine is paper only; Coinbase OAuth offers account-read access when approved partner credentials are configured. Stocks and forex remain strategy simulations. Live brokerage, custody, and withdrawals are not available.

## Brand Personality
Calm, precise, independent. A useful marketplace with credible detail and no profitability promises.

## Anti-references
No subscription upsells, invented verified returns, casino styling, or ambiguous live-trading controls. Avoid clutter, decorative glass, and excessive accent colors.

## Design Principles
Lead with the command desk and personal WhatsApp connection. Show pairing, delivery, worker availability, and broker capabilities honestly. Keep previews, confirmations, spending limits, activity history, and emergency stop easy to find. Distinguish paper cash from broker balances. Retain one-time version licensing, operating costs, and marketplace review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, semantic controls, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive mobile workflows.
